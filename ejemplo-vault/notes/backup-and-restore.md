# Backup and restore

A backup is a periodic snapshot of data you can restore after loss or corruption. Keep at
least three copies, on two different media, with one off-site (the 3-2-1 rule).

To restore: pick the most recent healthy snapshot, verify its checksum, restore to a staging
area first, then promote. Always test a restore before you need it — an untested backup is a
hope, not a guarantee. Schedule restores as drills, not just backups.
